--- UPDATE 2026-08-13: Re-QC and correction ---

the Set Origin/Reorient technique) was less precise than later,
practiced attempts. T1 header itself confirmed normal - not a
data-quality defect.

correct).

passes cleanly.

correction precision sensitivity - see docs/processing_journal.md
